About the Game

A puzzle game with a vacation feeling

Sunbay Shuffle combines classic matching mechanics with light building elements.
Each completed level helps restore parts of the resort — from beach bars to pools and activity zones.
You can actually see the resort coming back to life as you play, which is pretty satisfying.
